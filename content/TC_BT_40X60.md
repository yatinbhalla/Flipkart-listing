## Description (Additional Description > Description, max 5000 chars)

```
Keep your centre table protected without hiding it. This transparent black PVC table cover
measures 40 x 60 inches and is finished with a decorative golden lace border, so the surface
underneath stays visible while the top stays sealed against everyday damage.

What does it protect against?
Water and liquid spills, tea and coffee rings, oil and food stains, scratches from crockery,
keys and cutlery, heat marks from warm cups, and routine dust settling.

Who is it for?
Sized for 4 seater centre tables, coffee tables, side tables and study desks up to
40 x 60 inches. The clear finish suits wooden, glass and marble tops equally, which makes it
a practical choice for living rooms, offices, hostels and rented homes where the table itself
should still be seen.

How do you clean it?
Wipe with a soft damp cloth and let it air dry. No machine washing, no bleach, no ironing.
Because PVC does not absorb liquid, spills sit on the surface and lift off in a single wipe
instead of soaking in the way a fabric tablecloth does.

Why a PVC cover instead of a cloth tablecloth?
A fabric cloth absorbs whatever is spilled on it and then needs washing and drying before it
can go back on the table. A PVC sheet is wiped clean in seconds, does not stain from oil or
turmeric, does not need ironing, and does not fray at the edges. It also lies flat without
wrinkles straight out of the pack.

Specifications
Size: 40 x 60 inches (approximately 101 x 152 cm)
Material: PVC (polyvinyl chloride)
Colour: Transparent black with golden lace border
Pattern: Solid
Type: Table cover
Seating capacity: 4 seater
Reversible: No
Wrinkle free: Yes
Pack contents: 1 table cover

Care instructions
Wipe with a soft damp cloth. Do not machine wash. Do not bleach. Do not iron. Dry in shade.
```
